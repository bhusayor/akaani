1.	Introduction
1.1.	Akaani started with a simple question: what do you actually want to eat? Akaani Technologies Limited (‘Akaani’, ‘we’, ‘us’ or ‘our’) brings structure to African meals by helping you decide what to eat based on your preferences, goals and lifestyle, without losing the culture in the process. Through Lu, our AI guide, you go from not knowing what to eat to a clear, personalised answer in seconds. We also connect you, where you choose to use this feature, with independent Registered Dietitians for personalised nutrition consultations.
1.2.	This Privacy Policy (together with any other documents referred to in it) sets out the basis on which the personal data collected from you, or that you provide to us, is processed by us in connection with your use of our website, mobile application, Lu, our meal planning and recommendation tools, Akaani Meals, and our dietitian consultation services (together, the ‘Services’).
1.3.	We understand the confidential nature of the Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data we collect, including data about your health, dietary needs and eating goals, and are committed to protecting it.
1.4.	This Privacy Policy tells you what Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data we collect, how and why we collect and process it, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and what rights you have in relation to it.
1.5.	By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood and agree to this Privacy Policy, as it may be updated by us from time to time.
1.6.	At certain intervals, we may make some changes to these notices to meet changing circumstances, law, or any other triggering factor. Where any significant changes are made, we will notify you of such changes. Otherwise, you may track the changes we make from the headers of the notices, which disclose the version of the notice and the most recent updates.
1.7.	If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy or how we collect, process, or manage your personal data at Akaani, you may contact us at hello@useakaani.com

3.	What Data Do We Collect?

3.1.2.	Goals and preference data: your food preferences, cultural or cuisine preferences, and eating goals (for example, weight, protein or fitness-related goals) and other lifestyle information you share with Lu or otherwise input into the Platform to personalise your meal recommendations;
3.1.3.
