Is a dietitian the same as a nutritionist?

Not quite. A registered dietitian is trained, licensed and regulated to give nutrition advice for medical conditions, which is why they can work with things like diabetes, PCOS and hypertension. Anyone can call themselves a nutritionist.

I am not sick. Is this still for me?

Yes. Most people book for fat loss, muscle gain, energy, pregnancy, or simply to stop guessing at every meal. You do not need a diagnosis to want a plan that fits your body.

Do I need lab results before the call?

No. Bring them if you have them and your dietitian will use them, but the session works perfectly well without any.

Will I be told to stop eating Nigerian food?

No. That is the entire point of akaani. Your plan is built on the food you already cook and buy, adjusted in portion, pairing and frequency rather than replaced.

How do I pay and book?

Pick a time on Calendly and pay ₦50,000 once. Your confirmation and video link arrive by email straight away.

What if I need to reschedule?

Use the reschedule link in your confirmation email. There is no charge for moving your session.
